Privacy Policy

What personal data we handle, why we handle it and how you can exercise your rights.

Last updated: October 31, 2026.

1. Who we are

This website is operated by Parts Seals Vedações Industriais, Brazilian company registration (CNPJ) 30.705.918/0001-05, located at R. José Adhemar Petrini, 60 – Pq. Ind. Bandeirantes, Santa Bárbara d'Oeste – SP, Brazil, 13457-174. Under the LGPD we are the controller of the personal data described here.

2. Summary

No tracking, advertising or analytics cookies.

No forms send data to our servers: contact happens through WhatsApp, e-mail or phone, at your initiative.

We do not sell or share personal data for marketing purposes.

3. Data we handle and why

  • Sales and technical contact — when you reach us by WhatsApp, e-mail or phone we receive your name, phone, e-mail, company and whatever you send (photos, dimensions, drawings, application details), used to reply, quote, manufacture and deliver. Legal basis: pre-contractual steps and contract performance (art. 7, V) and legitimate interest (art. 7, IX).
  • Billing and shipping data — company name, tax ID, address and contacts needed for invoicing and delivery. Legal basis: contract performance and legal obligations (art. 7, II and V).
  • Technical access logs — our hosting provider automatically records IP address, date and time, page and browser type for security and operation. Legal basis: legal obligation (Brazilian Internet Civil Framework, art. 15) and legitimate interest.
  • Material selector and questions — processed in your own browser and never sent to us, unless you choose to open WhatsApp with the pre-filled message.
  • Shipping system — the /expedicao area is for internal staff and order label lookups. Device location is only requested during an active delivery route, and the camera only to scan labels.

4. Cookies

This website uses no tracking, advertising or audience analytics cookies, so no cookie consent banner is shown. If this changes, this policy will be updated and your consent requested before any collection.

5. Third parties

  • Netlify, Inc. — website hosting (servers in the USA and other countries).
  • WhatsApp / Meta — when you choose to contact us on WhatsApp.
  • E-mail provider — to receive and send messages.
  • Carriers — name, address and phone for deliveries.
  • Accountants and authorities — when required by law.

Links to Google Maps, Google Reviews and LinkedIn lead to third-party websites governed by their own policies.

6. International transfers

Some services above may store data outside Brazil. Such transfers follow art. 33 of the LGPD, with providers that adopt appropriate contractual and security safeguards.

7. Retention

  • Conversations and quotes that did not become orders: up to 2 years after the last contact.
  • Customer, order and invoice data: for the period required by tax and accounting law (usually 5 years).
  • Website access logs: at least 6 months, kept by the hosting provider.

8. Your rights

Under art. 18 of the LGPD you may request confirmation and access, correction, anonymization, blocking or deletion, portability, information about sharing, withdrawal of consent and objection to processing based on legitimate interest. We reply within 15 days. You may also file a complaint with the Brazilian Data Protection Authority (ANPD).
